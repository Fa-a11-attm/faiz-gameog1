How to Play

🍎 Red Apples (+10 pts)
Eat apples to grow your snake and increase your score. Every 5 apples eaten summons a special bonus!
⭐ Golden Bonus (+50 pts)
A shimmering star appears for 6 seconds. Grab it fast before the top countdown timer runs out!
💀 Collision Rules
Don't run into walls or bite your own tail. High score is saved automatically to your device.
📱 Controls
On desktop, use Arrow Keys or W/A/S/D. On mobile phones & tablets, swipe anywhere on the board or tap the touch buttons below.
